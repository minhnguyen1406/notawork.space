Talking

            

              
Hold V
Talk while you hold it

              
M or the Mic button
Leave the mic on; press again to mute

              
Camera button
Your face appears in a bubble over your head

              
Enter
Type in chat

              
I
Connection info, for when something looks wrong

            

            
Voices get quieter with distance: full volume within 3 metres, silent past 12. People sitting at the same table or round the fire always hear and see each other in full.

          

          

            
Watching together

            
Share screen puts a window or tab on the big cinema screen for everyone. Watch full screen fills your window with it; Esc closes. One person shares at a time.

            
To share sound, use Chrome or Edge, pick a browser tab and tick “share tab audio”. Netflix and similar services show a black screen when shared.

Claude

            
Claude lives by the fire and does what you ask in chat. It knows a few commands; it can't hold a conversation or hear voice.

            

              
/claude chess
Plays you at the chess table

              
/claude come · follow · stop
Walks to you, tags along, stays put

              
/claude go fire, cinema, kitchen, pitch, wheel
Sends it somewhere

              
/claude dance · wave · joke
For fun

              
/claude flip · roll · pick
Coin, die, or a random person

              
/claude bye
Sends it away; any /claude brings it back

            

          

          

            
Kitchen

            

              
E
Take an ingredient or plate, put things down, cook, serve

              
F
Chop at a chopping board

            

            
Chop tomato and lettuce, cook patties and eggs, build a dish on a plate and carry it to “Serve here”. The menu shows on the left while you are in the kitchen. Nothing is timed.

          

          

            
Outdoor games

            

              
Football
Walk or run into the ball to dribble it. Space shoots, harder if you're running. E at the scoreboard resets the score.

              
Lap race
E at Gate 1, then run through the gates in order.

              
Duel
Stand next to someone and press G. If they accept, you each pick Pillow, Rubber chicken or Umbrella with 1 2 3. Three hearts each.

              
Picker wheel
E beside it picks someone in the room at random.

            

          

          

            
Table games

            
Sit down with E and the game opens. Each one has its rules on screen. Stand up with E.

            

              
Chess
Two players, plus seats to watch

              
Last Card
Get rid of your cards first. Two or more players.

              
Poker
Texas Hold’em with play chips. Two or more players.

              
Werewolf
Five to twelve players round the far fire

              
Phố Lớn
Buy cities, collect rent. Two to six players.
